📏 Gap Tolerances (BS 8214)
+
Maximum permitted gaps between door leaf and frame per BS 8214:2016+A1:2021
LocationMax GapNotes
Sides & Top4mmFD30 & FD60
Threshold (no seal)8mmFD30 only
Threshold (with seal)3mmFD30S / FD60S
Meeting edges (double)4mmRebated or square
Key rule: Gaps must be as small as possible whilst allowing the door to close and latch freely without binding. Any gap exceeding these limits is a fail.
🔌 BM TRADA Q-Mark Plug Colours
+
Coloured plugs inserted into the door edge near the top hinge identify the fire rating and certification status.
Outer Colour — Fire Rating
Yellow
FD30 — 30 minutes
Blue
FD60 — 60 minutes
Brown
FD90 — 90 minutes
Black
FD120 — 120 minutes
Inner Tree Colour — Manufacture Status
Red — Door leaf or frame only, not in finished state. Further work required before hanging.
Green — Leaf or frame with intumescent seals supplied. Hardware prep may still be needed.
Orange — Glazing fitted to leaf by Q-Mark manufacturer. In addition to other plugs.
Silver — Complete factory-hung doorset. Ready for installation.
Gold — Installed by Q-Mark Approved Installer. Must only be on silver-plug doorsets.
The number in the centre of the plug is the certified company's unique ID number for traceability.

⚖️ Legal Requirements Summary
+
Key legal obligations under current UK fire safety legislation.
Buildings over 11m — Common Areas
Fire doors in common areas must be inspected at least every 3 months
Fire Safety (England) Regulations 2022 — Regulation 10
Buildings over 11m — Flat Entrance Doors
Responsible Person must use best endeavours to check flat entrance fire doors at least every 12 months
Fire Safety (England) Regulations 2022 — Regulation 10
High Traffic Areas
BS 8214 recommends a risk-based approach — high traffic doors may need inspection up to weekly
BS 8214:2016+A1:2021
Residents Information Duty
In any residential building with 2+ self-contained units, tenants must be given information on fire door importance annually and at start of tenancy
Fire Safety (England) Regulations 2022
Maintenance Duty
Fire doors are "equipment and devices" requiring a suitable system of maintenance under Article 17 of the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005
RRO 2005 — Article 17
